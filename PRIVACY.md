# CadetLinks Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 8, 2026

CadetLinks is intended for Detachment 785 cadets and staff. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and who can access it.

## Information We Collect and Store

CadetLinks stores information needed to provide app features, including:

- Account and profile information, such as name, email address, phone number, rank, class year, flight, and job/role
- Authentication data needed to sign in and manage account access
- Operational app data, including announcements, events, RSVPs, attendance records, and role permissions
- Documents uploaded by authorized users (for example PDFs or other files shared within the detachment)

## How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- Authenticate users and control access to the app
- Let cadets view detachment announcements, events, attendance, and shared resources
- Enable cadets to find and contact other cadets using profile contact fields
- Allow authorized roles to upload and manage documents
- Support administrator account creation and management

## Data Sharing

- We do not sell personal information.
- Cadet data is shared only within the CadetLinks system for approved detachment use.
- Profile and operational data is visible to authenticated users according to app permissions.
- Uploaded documents are available to users with access to the related in-app areas.

## Access Control and Security

- CadetLinks requires login for access to app data.
- Accounts are created by administrators.
- Role-based permissions are used to restrict certain actions (such as event creation, attendance editing, and document uploads).
- Data is stored using Firebase services (Authentication, Realtime Database, and Cloud Storage).

No system can guarantee absolute security, but we apply access controls appropriate to the app's use case.

## Data Retention

Cadet data is retained as long as needed for detachment operations, unless deleted or updated by authorized administrators.

## Children's Privacy

CadetLinks is not intended for children under 18.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.

## Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or requests related to your data, contact the CadetLinks administrators for Detachment 785.
